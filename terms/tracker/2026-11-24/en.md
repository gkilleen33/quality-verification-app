# PesaLog: Terms of Use and Privacy Notice

Version: 2026-11-24

## 1. PesaLog is a research app

PesaLog is a mobile app developed by the Development Innovation Lab (DIL) at the University of Chicago for an academic research study of furniture businesses in Kenya [study title; IRB protocol number]. PesaLog exists to record the mobile money payments of people who take part in that study. Recording those payments for research is the purpose of the app. PesaLog has no commercial purpose.

You must read and accept these terms before you use PesaLog. If you do not accept them, you cannot use the app. Using PesaLog is voluntary. [Whether declining or stopping affects participation in the study or use of Fundi Bora.]

## 2. What PesaLog does

- PesaLog reads the notifications your phone shows when a mobile money message arrives from M-Pesa or Airtel Money.
- From each notification it records one payment: the amount, the time, the type, the fee, your balance afterwards, and who the payment was with.
- It shows you a log of money coming in and going out, with totals. You can add a category and a comment to each payment.
- If you agree, it sends these records to DIL's research server.

PesaLog does not read your SMS inbox. It cannot read messages that arrived before you installed it, or notifications you dismissed while it was off.

## 3. Notification access

PesaLog needs a phone permission called notification access. Android gives an app with this permission the ability to see every notification on the phone. PesaLog uses only notifications of mobile money messages from M-Pesa and Airtel Money. It ignores every other notification: it does not store them, and it does not send them anywhere.

You can turn notification access off at any time in your phone's settings. PesaLog then stops reading.

## 4. Who may use PesaLog

- You must be 18 or older.
- You must be taking part in the study, with an account DIL gave you or your Fundi Bora account.
- Each person is limited to one account. You are not permitted to share your account.

## 5. Information PesaLog collects

**Kept on your phone, once you agree to reading:**

- for each payment: the amount, time, type, fee, your balance afterwards, the transaction code, the network (M-Pesa or Airtel Money), and the name or phone number of the other person. PesaLog keeps the name and number only to show you who each payment was with, so you can follow how your business is doing;
- the category and comment you add.

**Sent to DIL's server, only if you also agree to sending:**

- the same details of each payment, and your categories and comments;
- who it was with: PesaLog sends the other person's name or number to the server so that the server can replace them with secure codes, made from the name, the number, and part of the number. The server never stores the real name or phone number;
- for each day, the number of mobile money messages PesaLog could not read;
- the app version, to run and fix the service.

## 6. How DIL uses the information

DIL uses the information for academic research only: to study how money moves through furniture businesses, and to produce research papers and reports. Published results show totals and averages across many people. They never name you or show your individual payments.

If you also use Fundi Bora, DIL links your PesaLog records to your Fundi Bora account for the same research.

DIL does not:

- sell your information or use it for advertising;
- use it to decide whether you receive credit, a loan, or any service;
- share it with Safaricom, Airtel, a lender, or a buyer;
- send it to an artificial-intelligence model.

DIL shares information with others only when the law requires it. [Whether records with names removed will be shared with other researchers or a data archive.]

## 7. Who else handles the information

- **Amazon Web Services** hosts the server that stores your records, in the United States.
- **DIL research staff** are bound by confidentiality and use your records for the research in section 6.

## 8. Your choices

- **Stop reading:** turn off "Read mobile money notifications" in Settings. PesaLog stops recording payments.
- **Stop sending:** turn off "Send to the research team" in Settings. PesaLog stops sending, and your records stay on your phone.
- **Delete your records from the server:** tap "Delete my records from the server" in Settings. DIL deletes every payment record PesaLog sent from your account. DIL keeps only the date of the deletion and the number of records deleted.
- **Delete the records on your phone:** uninstall PesaLog.

None of these choices costs you anything. [Any effect on study payments.]

## 9. How long the information is kept

- DIL keeps your records on the server until you delete them, or until [retention period after the study ends].
- Backup copies of the server are kept for up to 30 days, so deleted records may remain in a backup for up to 30 days.

## 10. Sending your information out of Kenya

PesaLog sends your payment records to Amazon Web Services in the United States. Amazon Web Services has written terms that require it to keep your information secure and use it only to provide its service.

This transfer has risks:

- US law may not protect personal data to the same standard as Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.
- US authorities may be able to require the company to hand over data under US law.
- It may be harder to enforce your rights against a company in the United States.

You can withdraw your agreement at any time by turning off sending in Settings.

## 11. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

- be told how your personal data is used (this notice);
- see the personal data DIL holds about you;
- object to the use of all or part of your data;
- ask DIL to correct data that is false or misleading;
- ask DIL to delete your data;
- withdraw your agreement to sending your information out of Kenya (section 10).

To use these rights, contact DIL (section 14). If you are not satisfied with DIL's answer, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke). [IRB contact for questions about your rights as a research participant.]

## 12. Security and cost

DIL protects your information with encrypted connections, scrambled passwords, and a server that only the people who run PesaLog can reach. PesaLog is free. Your mobile network may charge for data.

## 13. Limited responsibility, changes, and ending

- PesaLog's log may be incomplete or wrong. It misses a payment when the notification is switched off, shortened, or dismissed. It is not a bank statement or an accounting record. To the extent the law allows, DIL and the University of Chicago are not responsible for any loss that comes from relying on it.
- DIL may change these terms. When they change, the app shows the new terms, and you must accept them to continue.
- You may stop using PesaLog at any time.
- DIL may change, pause, or end PesaLog.
