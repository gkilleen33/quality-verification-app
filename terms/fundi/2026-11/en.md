# Fundi Bora: Terms of Use and Privacy Notice

Version: 2026-11

## 1. About these terms

Fundi Bora is a mobile app developed by the Development Innovation Lab (DIL) at the University of Chicago. These terms explain how you may use Fundi Bora and what happens to the information you give it. You must read and accept these terms before you use Fundi Bora. If you do not accept them, you cannot use the app.

## 2. What Fundi Bora does

Fundi Bora gives carpenters (fundis) feedback on the furniture they make. It sends your photos and messages to an artificial-intelligence model, Claude, made by Anthropic. Claude looks at the photos and writes an assessment and advice.

The assessments come from a computer model. They can be wrong or incomplete. They are not a guarantee of quality, a safety certificate, or professional advice. Use your own judgement before you act on them, and before you sell or repair a piece. DIL and Anthropic are not responsible for poor advice in an assessment, or for any damage to a piece, lost sale, or other loss that comes from following it.

## 3. Who may use Fundi Bora

- You must be 18 or older.
- You need a phone number that can receive SMS or WhatsApp messages.
- Each person is limited to one account. You are not permitted to share your account or use another person's account.

## 4. Your account

- Register with your real name and your own phone number.
- Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens in your account.
- If you think someone else has used your account, or you have a question about Fundi Bora, contact DIL at nshaddon@uchicago.edu.

## 5. Information Fundi Bora collects

- **Name and phone number,** when you create an account, to identify your account and contact you.
- **Password,** when you create an account, to protect it. The server stores it in hashed form only.
- **Photos of furniture,** with each assessment, to produce the assessment.
- **Photo details,** with each photo: when it was taken; the phone's make and model and its camera settings; the serial numbers of the camera and lens, and the photo's own ID number; any owner or author name the phone recorded; the photo's original size; and, only while the location switch in Settings is on, the GPS position the camera recorded. These are used to judge photo quality and to check that the app works correctly.
- **Your messages and Claude's replies,** with each assessment, to produce the assessment and keep your history.
- **Your workshop location,** when you set up the app, to place your workshop in the study area.
- **A GPS point for each assessment,** only while the location switch in Settings is on, to record where the work was assessed.
- **Names of workers you add,** to record who worked on each piece.
- **Tools you list,** to tailor advice to the tools you have.
- **App version and usage records** (the time, length, and cost of each request), to run, fix, and pay for the service.

Your name, phone number, password, and photos are needed to use Fundi Bora. Everything else is optional: you can leave it out or turn it off, and the app still works.

Fundi Bora does not read your SMS, contacts, or other apps.

## 6. How DIL uses the information

DIL uses the information to:

- produce assessments and advice, and keep your history in the app;
- run and secure Fundi Bora;
- improve the app's functionality, including the instructions given to Claude;
- contact you about your account or the app.

DIL does not sell your information and does not use it for advertising.

## 7. Who else handles the information

- **Anthropic** runs Claude and is based in the United States. It receives your photos and messages to write each reply, and handles them on DIL's behalf under its terms for business customers. Under those terms, Anthropic does not use them to train its models, and it deletes them within 30 days. Anthropic may keep them for up to 2 years if its automated safety systems flag them as breaking its usage policy, and for longer where the law requires.
- **Amazon Web Services** hosts the server that stores your account and assessments, in the United States.
- **DIL staff** are bound by confidentiality and handle your data to improve the app's functionality.

Because Anthropic and Amazon Web Services are in the United States, using Fundi Bora transfers your personal data out of Kenya. Your data travels to both over encrypted connections. Section 14 explains the risks of this transfer and asks for your agreement.

DIL shares information with others only when the law requires it.

## 8. Your photos and Fundi Bora's code

You keep ownership of your photos. By uploading them, you allow DIL to store and use them to provide and improve Fundi Bora.

Fundi Bora's code is open source under the MIT licence. The Fundi Bora name and DIL's logo belong to DIL.

## 9. How long the information is kept

- You can delete an assessment in the app. It disappears from your phone at once and is deleted from the server within 7 days.
- You can delete your account in Settings. DIL then removes your name, phone number, password, workshop location, the GPS points of your assessments, your piece names, and your workers' names, and your account cannot be used again. Your photos and assessments are kept without your name.
- Backup copies of the server are kept for up to 30 days, so deleted information may remain in a backup for up to 30 days.

## 10. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

- be told how your personal data is used (this notice);
- see the personal data DIL holds about you;
- object to the use of all or part of your data;
- ask DIL to correct data that is false or misleading;
- ask DIL to delete data that is false or misleading, or that DIL is no longer allowed to keep;
- withdraw your agreement to sending your information out of Kenya (section 14).

To use these rights, contact DIL (section 15). If you are not satisfied with DIL's answer, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

## 11. Security

DIL protects your information with encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and restricted access to the server.

## 12. Cost

Fundi Bora is free. Your mobile network may charge for data.

## 13. Limited responsibility, changes, and ending

- Fundi Bora may provide incorrect information. To the extent the law allows, DIL and the University of Chicago are not responsible for any loss that comes from using Fundi Bora's assessments.
- DIL may change these terms. When they change, the app shows the new terms, and you must accept them to continue.
- DIL may suspend or close an account that misuses Fundi Bora or breaks these terms.
- You may stop using Fundi Bora and delete your account at any time.
- DIL may change, pause, or end Fundi Bora.

## 14. Sending your information out of Kenya

Fundi Bora sends your information, including photos of your furniture and your workshop location, to Anthropic and Amazon Web Services in the United States. Amazon Web Services and Anthropic each have written terms that require them to keep your information secure and use it only to provide their service.

This transfer has risks:

- US law may not protect personal data to the same standard as Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.
- US authorities may be able to require these companies to hand over data under US law.
- It may be harder to enforce your rights against a company in the United States.

Fundi Bora cannot work without this transfer. You can withdraw your agreement at any time in Settings. If you withdraw it, you can no longer use Fundi Bora.
